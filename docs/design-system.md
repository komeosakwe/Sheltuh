## The system in one screen

- **Colour** (`app/globals.css`; Light / Dark, and the ink scope takes the Dark values):
  - surface/page `--background` #fff / #000; text/primary and border/strong `--foreground` #000 / #fff;
  - surface/muted `--surface` #f2f1ee / #1f1f1f; border/subtle `--surface-border` #e5e3de / #333 (1px dividers only);
  - text/secondary `--muted` #5c5c5c / #a3a3a3; text/disabled `--disabled` #a3a3a3 / #5c5c5c;
  - surface/card-hover `--hover` (`bg-hover`) cream #fffae7 / #1a1a1a: the hover fill of cards and filters, never a default fill. One exception: /community's cream panels (Local discovery, Your visibility) use it as their fill, so the cards on them are the outlined `surface="cream"` variant, whose hover is `--surface`.
  - **Signal Orange #ff4800** (`--signal`) is for the main action and the two-dot motif only, always with black text (6.18:1). Never small orange text on white, never an orange border or focus ring.
  - Category tints (`CategoryTag`, `[data-tag]`): live music, art, workshop, pop-up, theatre, fashion (defined, no category yet), free; each has a Light pair and a Dark pair (Dark and ink), each at least 4.5:1 (`tests/tag-contrast.test.ts`). Tags only, never section or button fills.
- **Type** (`app/fonts.ts`): **Bootzy** (`--face-display`) for display headlines only, Regular with no synthesised bold and no size-adjusted fallback (missing glyphs fall to Instrument Sans); **Instrument Sans** for UI, headings, body, buttons and nav; Instrument Sans for the facts and labels too (dates, times, places, eyebrows), as written in sentence case with normal tracking (owner decision, 13 Nov 2026); **IBM Plex Mono** (`font-mono`, not preloaded) only for machine codes: ticket codes, order references, the door's code entry and admin token links. The fifteen styles are utilities: `display-xl` / `display-l` / `display-m` (Bootzy, capitals), `heading-h1` … `heading-h4`, `body-l` / `body-m` / `body-s`, `label-nav` (16/24 Medium, Round 2), `label-button`, `label-tag`, `mono-m`, `mono-s`. Display and Heading step down below 768px. Round 2 adds three: `display-hero` (the home hero's brand line: Bootzy capitals 40/48, 48/56 from 768px, 56/64 from 1280px), `title-banner` (an event's title on the featured banner: Bootzy **as entered**, 28/32, 32/40, 40/48) and `heading-section-l` (home and /community section titles: Instrument Sans 700, 24/32, 32/40 from 768px). Event titles, venues and addresses are never upper-cased by CSS (`event-title` included); capitals are for brand lines and short labels. The old names point at them: `display-lg` = heading-h1 (sentence case), `display-md` = heading-h3, `title-lg` = display-m, `eyebrow` = Instrument Sans 500 12/16 in sentence case, `meta` = mono-s. `mono-m` / `mono-s` keep the Type board's names but are Instrument Sans 400 with `tabular-nums`.
- **Ü**: only in display headlines, through `BrandU` (shown "FIND YOÜR NEXT THING.", read "Find your next thing."). The dotted body face and `DottedLine` are gone.
- **Shape**: radius 8 (`rounded-control`, `rounded-badge`) for things you tap and for tags, 16 (`rounded-card`, `rounded-sheet`, `rounded-panel`) for things you look at. People's avatars are round. No pills (the Google button keeps its own, per Google's rules) and no shadows. Chips, filters, inputs and secondary buttons have the 1.5px foreground stroke (`border-control`, `--stroke-control`).
- **Focus**: the global `:focus-visible` ring, 2px `--foreground` at a 3px offset: black on white, white in Dark and inside ink, so it shows around black and orange buttons. Every hover state has a `:focus-visible` twin (and, where it's press feedback, an `:active` one); nothing is shown on hover only. `.btn-signal` turns black with an orange label on keyboard focus as on hover.
- **Layout**: an 8px scale; `page-container` is the 1312px content column with 16 / 32 / 64px margins below 768 / from 768 / from 1280. The header (`--header-h`) is 56px below 1024px and 72px from it; from 1280px its row tightens to 64px once the page has scrolled (`data-scrolled`), while `--header-h` stays 72. Home and /community sections are 64 / 48 / 40px apart (from 1024 / from 768 / phones). DOM order equals visual order at every width: no `order-*` reshuffles.
- **Motion**: hover changes colour over 200ms ease-out; no lift and no scale. Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Components (`components/ui/`, `components/brand/`)

| Component | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `Button`, `ButtonLink`, `buttonClass` | `signal` (Primary: orange, black label; black with an orange label on hover; one per view, never in checkout, forms, dashboards or admin), `outline` (Secondary: 1.5px stroke; black with a white label on hover), `solid` (black), `danger`. Sizes `sm` 40, `md` 48 (default), `lg` 56. Label/Button, sentence case. Disabled is grey. CSS in `.btn*`. `secondaryActionClass` is the Secondary look for an icon + label action. |
| `FilterChip`, `filterChipClass` | A toggle chip (`aria-pressed`): 48px, 1.5px stroke, radius 8; cream on hover; foreground-filled when selected, with the two orange dots. `wrap` (a long list of chips in a form: the dashboard's profile categories) makes it at least 48px, its label free to wrap at large text, never wider than its row. |
| `FilterDropdown` | A native `<select>` dressed as a filter: 48px, 1.5px stroke; cream on hover; foreground-filled when `active`. Named by `label`. |
| `CategoryTag` | A non-interactive tag in its category's tint (`tone`: a category, `free` or `neutral`): radius 8, 24px, Label/Tag. `size="md"` (at least 32px, Body S 500, 12px each side; wraps onto more lines at large text rather than truncating) is for the organiser profile's chips only. |
| `DateChip` | The card's date block (weekday, day, month in the event's zone), white with black text in both themes. Decorative. |
| `PriceTag` | The card's price label: 40px, Label/Button, orange with a black label, or `neutral`. On card hover or keyboard focus on the card's link it turns black with an orange label (as Buy tickets' hover); `neutral` doesn't change. Never a link, button or tab stop. "Free + paid" when a paid ticket is on sale beside a free one (`formatCardPriceLabel`). |
| `GoingRow` | A card's going line: small overlapping round faces (a photo where the payload has one, else a colour dot; no initials) and "N going" on one line. No "(sample)": the demo banner says it. |
| `EventCard`, `EventCardPlaceholder`, `CardPrice` | The one event card: a 2:1 image (radius 16; a portrait poster shown whole on its blur) with Save; the tags (category, "Sold out", "Ended", "Reserved seating", a trending qualifier; no "Free" tag, the price says it); the title (heading-h4, two lines); two meta lines in Instrument Sans, as written, each cut short to one line (Mono/S on a narrow card): when, "Sat 14 Nov · 5pm" (`cardWhenParts`), and where, "Coburg, VIC · The Depot Theatre" (`cardWhereLabel`); then `GoingRow` and the all-inclusive `CardPrice variant="tag"`, on one line from a 16rem card and stacked below that. The title's link is the card's only link, stretched over it; Save (and a clip's Play) sit above it as siblings. Hover or keyboard focus: a cream panel, a title underline and the price label inverted; a touch press shows the panel. `surface="cream"` (on a cream panel): a white card with the 1.5px stroke that turns `--surface` on hover, focus or press, its ring on the card, 16px inside. Sold out: the image at 60%. No ticket button and no "By organiser" (both are on the event page). Labels from `lib/event-card.ts`. |
| `InitialsAvatar` | Initials in a circle; `you` is orange with black initials. |
| `Page`, `PageHeader`, `Strap` | Page container; page header (mono strap + h1: `display-lg`, or `display-l` for a `brand` line) |
| `SectionHead` | The one section head (home, profiles, Saved): `display-md` h2, optional note and link. |
| `BackLink`, `Panel`, `EmptyState`, `Notice` | As before. |
| `BrandU`, `Wordmark`, `UMark`, `DotsMark`, `DotsLoader` (`components/brand/`) | The brand's Ü line, logo vectors, the two-dot motif and loader. |
| `FeaturedCarousel` (`components/home/`) | The home's featured banner (Round 2 §1.1): 4:5 below 640px, 2:1 to 1023px, 5:2 from 1024px; the photo under one gradient (clear to 35%, 80% black at the bottom), no box; category, the title (`title-banner`, as entered) whose link is stretched over the slide, "date · venue", and the ticket action as a raised sibling (M below 1440px, L from it). Pause/Play then the dots on a white pill, bottom-right at every width, before the slides in the DOM. Autoplays every 6s; holds on hover and on focus inside it (but on Pause), stops on touch, a dot or a swipe; never under reduced motion, where there's no Pause. |
| `SearchBar` (`components/`) | The inline bar (words, Where, Search) from 1024px; below it one field that opens the full-screen search sheet. |

Organiser profile (`components/organisers/`, `docs/design/organiser-profile.md`): `OrganiserCover` is the organiser's photo across the top, a 7:2 frame (`rounded-card`, `bg-surface` while loading or if it fails) at least 176px tall, so phones get a taller centre crop; nothing is written on it and it's decorative. The logo (`OrganiserLogo size="profile"`: 96 / 128 / 160) sits over its bottom-left corner, cut out by a 4px page-colour ring; without a cover it's in the flow, without a logo nothing stands in. Below, in one column and DOM order: the strap and name, a line of facts (events, followers from 10, member since), Follow (`signal`, the page's one orange) with Share, the chips (`CategoryTag tone="neutral" size="md"`), bio and links (beside them from 1024px), upcoming events (`HOME_GRID`), the featured video, past events as a ruled date | title | place list (up to 12) and "Report this organiser".

Favourites (`components/favourites/`): `SaveButton` is a toggle with a fixed name and `aria-pressed` (a sign-in link when signed out). On a card (`overlay`) it's a 32px white square in a 48px target (40px from 768px); saved, the heart fills orange.

## Review gates (tests)

- `tests/brand-tokens.test.ts`: the token values, radii, faces, type styles and buttons above.
- `tests/tag-contrast.test.ts`: every category tag pair is at least 4.5:1 in Light and Dark.
- `tests/bootzy-font.test.ts`: the Bootzy file and README are present, loaded with `adjustFontFallback: false`, and the display stack ends in Instrument Sans.
- `tests/door-tokens.test.ts`: the door palette stays scoped to the scanner.
- `tests/pwa-manifest.test.ts`: `APP_THEME_COLOR` equals the first `--background` in `globals.css`.
